# HR Analytics — Executive Summary

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition using SQL Server and Power BI, with AI-assisted interpretation to translate workforce data into actionable business insights.

The objective is to understand employee attrition patterns, identify workforce segments requiring further investigation, and recommend data-driven retention strategies.

## 2. Tools and Technologies

- **SQL Server:** Data validation, cleaning, aggregation, and business analysis.
- **Power BI:** KPI reporting, interactive dashboards, and workforce analysis.
- **AI-assisted analysis:** Summarizing validated findings and developing evidence-based recommendations.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation, SQL scripts, and portfolio presentation.

## 3. Workforce Overview

The dataset contains 1,470 employee records.

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Employees | 1,470 |
| Employees Who Left | 237 |
| Employees Who Stayed | 1,233 |
| Overall Attrition Rate | 16.12% |

*These figures are dataset-level benchmarks and should be checked against the final SQL results.*

## 4. Analytical Focus Areas

The analysis examines employee attrition across:

- Department and job role
- Overtime status
- Job satisfaction
- Work-life balance
- Age groups and tenure

These comparisons help identify employee segments with relatively high attrition rates and guide further investigation.

## 5. Business Recommendations

Potential retention actions to evaluate against the analysis results include:

1. **Review high-attrition job roles:** Investigate workload, career progression, compensation, and management practices in roles with elevated attrition.
2. **Evaluate overtime patterns:** Examine whether employees working overtime have higher attrition rates and assess workload distribution.
3. **Strengthen employee experience:** Review job satisfaction and work-life balance patterns alongside attrition.
4. **Support employee development:** Evaluate career progression and tenure patterns to identify opportunities for better engagement and retention.
5. **Monitor workforce KPIs:** Track attrition rate, employee exits, and segment-level trends through the Power BI dashboard.

These are recommendations to investigate, not confirmed causes of attrition.

## 6. Conclusion

This project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow, from data validation and cleaning in SQL Server to interactive reporting in Power BI and AI-assisted business interpretation.

The goal is to help HR stakeholders move from descriptive reporting toward evidence-based workforce decisions.

## 7. Responsible Use of AI

AI is used to assist with summarization, interpretation, and recommendation drafting. Numerical findings should be verified against SQL queries and dashboard results. AI-generated explanations are not treated as proof of causation, and recommendations should be evaluated using relevant organizational evidence.
